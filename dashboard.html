Panel 1: NQ Live Chart

A real-time 5-minute candlestick chart of the selected futures contract, powered by TradingView. Each candle represents 5 minutes of price action. Green candles = price closed higher than it opened. Red candles = closed lower. Use the chart to identify swing highs and swing lows -- the structural pivots where Stan looks for divergence signals.

Panel 2: S949 Oscillator

Stan's primary decision instrument. The oscillator runs on a ±40 scale. The dashed red line at 0 is the zero line -- the neutral midpoint between bullish and bearish momentum. Green segments indicate rising momentum; red indicates falling.

What to look for: Divergence between price swing points and oscillator values at those same points. When the two slopes point in opposite directions, divergence is present.

Type 2 (Stan's preferred): With-trend. Uptrend: price higher low, oscillator lower low = Type 2 BUY. Downtrend: price lower high, oscillator higher high = Type 2 SELL.

Type 1: Against-trend reversal attempt. Higher high in price + lower high in oscillator = Type 1 SELL. Lower low in price + higher low in oscillator = Type 1 BUY.

Oscillator color: green = above zero, red = below zero. BUY/SELL arrows mark confirmed divergence points. The signal is the slope comparison, not the oscillator color alone.

Note: Phase 1 oscillator is a realistic simulation. Stan's actual S949 is a proprietary NinjaTrader indicator. Live integration planned for Phase 2.
