01 - The Edge

Two layers. One system. Thirty years of proof.

Stan's system has never been documented - until now. It operates through two instruments he trusts above everything else, each answering a different question at a different resolution.

Layer 01 - Primary Signal

S949 Cyclical Momentum Oscillator

Developed by Rob Mitchell - one of the largest S&P futures traders of the 1980s. Stan received it personally. The S949 measures momentum's rate of change across the market's natural cycle. Its signature insight: price and momentum diverge before a reversal confirms.

Stan's analogy: a rocket reaching its apex still appears to be climbing - but the fuel is spent. The oscillator shows this before price does.

Layer 02 - Real-Time Confirmation

TickStrike Order Flow Meter

FinancialJuice's TickStrike captures what's happening inside the market right now - before the candle closes. Visual (green = buying, red = selling) and audio (sparse ticks vs machine-gun cadence). Stan: "Nothing tells you what's going on right this second except order flow."

What it's detecting: institutional algorithmic activity. BofA, Wells Fargo hedging their books. The big money before it shows up in price.

Signal Interpretation

The composite read. Every second.

The oscillator determines the structural setup. TickStrike confirms or contradicts it in real time. The intersection of both defines the signal state.

Confirmed

Oscillator BUY + TickStrike 10-14 in same direction. Both layers agree. Highest-probability entry. This is when Stan enters.

Conflicted

Oscillator gives a signal but TickStrike is pressing the opposite direction. Wait or reduce size. Real-time flow is fighting the structural setup.

Neutral

TickStrike below 9. Low conviction on both sides. No decisive institutional pressure. Signals generated here are unconfirmed - avoid.

07 -- Platform Deep Dive

Take Profit Trader: How Stan's Evaluation Works

Stan currently trades on Take Profit Trader (TPT), a Florida-based prop firm founded in 2021. Understanding how TPT works explains the financial context behind Stan's trading discipline.

The Three-Phase Journey

Phase 1 -- Test
Prove You Can Trade
Hit the profit target while following risk rules. Stan's $150K account: $9,000 target, $360/mo (or ~$180 with promo), max 15 contracts. Minimum 5 trading days. No time limit.
Phase 2 -- PRO
Funded Account, 80/20 Split
Simulated account backed by TPT capital. 80% of profits to trader, 20% to TPT. Intraday trailing drawdown (the hardest mechanic). Day-one withdrawals once buffer is cleared. Up to 3 resets at ~$199 each.
Phase 3 -- PRO+
Live Exchange, 90/10 Split
Real orders go to the CME exchange. End-of-day (not intraday) trailing drawdown -- far more forgiving. 90% of profits to trader. Auto-promotion implemented March 2026 for consistent traders.

The Intraday Trailing Drawdown

The most challenging PRO rule. The drawdown floor tracks your peak equity in real time -- including unrealized gains. If a trade temporarily reaches +$3,000 unrealized, the floor immediately rises by $3,000. Even if you close at +$1,000 net, you now have $2,000 less room than when you started.

This is why precise entry timing matters. The oscillator + TickStrike system reduces the number of false entries that create these unrealized swings.

Why TPT Is Manual-Only for Stan

TPT explicitly prohibits automated or bot trading (Universal Trading Policy 1). All trades must be manually executed. Stan's manual trading continues on TPT. The Tribune Trader automation system will run on a separate, automation-compatible platform: Topstep, Apex Trader Funding, or Earn2Trade.

09 -- Reference

Glossary

Every term used across this site, defined. Hover over highlighted terms anywhere on the page for instant definitions. Full definitions here for reference.

Trading Terms
S949
Stan's primary oscillator. Developed by Rob Mitchell. Runs on a +/-40 scale inside NinjaTrader. Core signal: divergence between oscillator and price.
TickStrike
Real-time order flow meter by FinancialJuice. Green = buying, red = selling. Scale 0-16+. Stan's sweet spot: 11-14. Two channels: visual and audio.
Divergence
When price and oscillator move in opposite directions at comparable swing points. The core trade signal in Stan's system. Price goes higher, but momentum goes lower -- the fuel is spent.
Type 2 Divergence
With-trend divergence. Stan's preferred setup. In an uptrend: price higher low, oscillator lower low. Entry at signal candle close. Target: prior swing high.
Gann Swing
Stan's method for identifying pivot points. More structurally precise than standard zigzag. A bar qualifies as a swing high/low only when higher/lower than both adjacent bars.
Trailing Drawdown
Dynamic maximum loss limit that follows peak balance upward. At TPT PRO: tracks unrealized peak equity in real time (intraday). Once the floor reaches starting balance, it locks permanently.
Order Flow
The real-time balance of aggressive buying vs. aggressive selling. TickStrike measures this. Reflects what institutional algorithms are doing at this exact moment.
NQ / ES / MNQ
NQ = E-mini NASDAQ-100 (1 point = $20). ES = E-mini S&P 500 (1 point = $50). MNQ = Micro NASDAQ-100 (1 point = $2, 1/10 of NQ). Stan trades NQ.
Prop Firm
Proprietary trading firm. Funds traders to trade on their behalf. Evaluation period required. Profit split on funded accounts (80/20 at TPT PRO). Capital at risk belongs to the firm.
Tech + Platform Terms
NinjaTrader
Professional futures trading platform. Stan's platform since 2003. Includes NinjaScript, REST API, WebSocket data, and MCP server. Free for charting and backtesting.
NinjaScript
C#-based programming language in NinjaTrader. Used to read Stan's oscillator values and compute order flow signals. The foundation of Tribune Trader's Phase 2 signal extraction.
OrderFlowCumulativeDelta
Native NinjaScript method computing the difference between aggressive buying and selling volume per bar. The technical equivalent of TickStrike's signal, built into the platform.
MCP Server
Model Context Protocol server. NinjaTrader's AI integration layer. Allows AI agents (like TRIBUNE) to place orders, query account state, and monitor positions via structured tool calls.
REST API
NinjaTrader's web API for programmatic order management. Authenticated with tokens. Automated orders use isAutomated=true flag. Swagger-documented.
Screen Capture (Data Collection)
Phase 1 data collection method: Stan's screen recorded every 2 seconds during live trading. Captures oscillator readings, TickStrike levels, and decisions for AI training.
TradingView Widget
Free embeddable chart widget providing live futures data. Powers the NQ chart on this dashboard. Symbol: CME_MINI:NQ1! for continuous NQ futures.
Ollama (Local AI)
Local AI model runtime for Llama/Mistral/other open models. Key to the Private/Local training route -- Stan's methodology stays on our hardware, never sent to external APIs.
TRIBUNE (The Agent)
The AI trading agent being built by Tribune Inc. Will receive oscillator + order flow signals from NinjaScript and execute trades autonomously on an automation-permitted prop platform.
