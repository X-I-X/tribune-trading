01 — The Edge

Two layers. One system. Thirty years of proof.

Stan's system has never been documented — until now. It operates through two instruments he trusts above everything else, each answering a different question at a different resolution.

Layer 01 — Primary Signal

S949 Cyclical Momentum Oscillator

Developed by Rob Mitchell — one of the largest S&P futures traders of the 1980s. Stan received it personally. The S949 measures momentum's rate of change across the market's natural cycle. Its signature insight: price and momentum diverge before a reversal confirms.

Stan's analogy: a rocket reaching its apex still appears to be climbing — but the fuel is spent. The oscillator shows this before price does.

+40 to +20 Strong Bullish
+20 to 0 Weakening Bullish
0 Neutral / Transition
0 to -20 Weakening Bearish
-20 to -40 Strong Bearish

Layer 02 — Real-Time Confirmation

TickStrike Order Flow Meter

FinancialJuice's TickStrike captures what's happening inside the market right now — before the candle closes. Visual (green = buying, red = selling) and audio (sparse ticks vs machine-gun cadence). Stan: "Nothing tells you what's going on right this second except order flow."

What it's detecting: institutional algorithmic activity. BofA, Wells Fargo hedging their books. The big money before it shows up in price.

Signal Interpretation

The composite read. Every second.

The oscillator determines the structural setup. TickStrike confirms or contradicts it in real time. The intersection of both defines the signal state.

Confirmed

Oscillator BUY + TickStrike 10-14 in same direction. Both layers agree. Highest-probability entry. This is when Stan enters.

Conflicted

Oscillator gives a signal but TickStrike is pressing the opposite direction. Wait or reduce size. Real-time flow is fighting the structural setup.

Neutral

TickStrike below 9. Low conviction on both sides. No decisive institutional pressure. Signals generated here are unconfirmed — avoid.
